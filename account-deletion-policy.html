Account Deletion Policy

Brawnax is a fitness mobile application where users create personal profiles. We respect user privacy and provide clear and simple options to delete accounts and associated personal data at any time.

1. What Data Will Be Deleted?

When a user deletes their account, the following information will be permanently removed from our servers and databases:

  • Username
  • Email address
  • Password
  • Profile details (age, weight, height, fitness goals)
  • Saved workouts or diet logs
  • App usage data
  • Any other personal information stored in the app

Once deleted, this data cannot be recovered.

2. How Can Users Delete Their Account?

A. Inside the App

Users can delete their account directly from the app:

Settings → Account → Delete My Account (Coming Soon)

After confirmation, the user’s account and all associated data will be permanently deleted from our database.

B. By Requesting via Email

Users may also request account deletion by sending an email to:

Email: support@brawnax.com
Email Subject: Delete My Brawnax Account

The request must be sent from the registered email address or the registered email must be clearly mentioned in the message.

We will process the account deletion request within 7 days.

3. What Happens After Account Deletion?

  • User login access is permanently disabled
  • All personal data is removed from our systems
  • Users may create a new account in the future if they wish

4. Retained Data

We do not retain any personal data after account deletion, except where required by law for:

  • Fraud prevention
  • Legal or regulatory compliance

5. Policy Updates

Brawnax may update this Account Deletion Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page.
